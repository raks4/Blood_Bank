Blood-Bank Management SystemProject Execution Guidelines/Plan:1.       Initially we have to brief about the project to the freshers and prepare the plan.2.       We have to divided them into team of 2 and identify a person to lead the team.3.       Guide them to divide the project into modules.4.       Guide them to create a project roadmap / schedule with a deadline.5.       Track the progress on daily basis so that project will be completed on given timeline.Project Skeleton:Problem Statement:Project Name – Blood Bank Management ServiceDescription of Problem StatementThe basic building aim is to provide blood donation service to the city recently. Designed to store, process, retrieve and analyze information concerned with the administrative and inventory management within a blood bank. Features / Modules to be ImplementedDonor ManagementRegister and maintain donor details.Search, view, update, and delete donor records.Maintain blood group and donation history.Blood Donation ManagementRecord new blood donations.Track donation date and donated blood units.Update inventory automatically after donation.Blood Inventory ManagementMaintain blood stock based on blood groups.Track available, reserved, and issued blood units.Identify low-stock and out-of-stock blood groups.Blood Request ManagementRegister blood requests from patients/hospitals.Check blood availability for requested blood groups.Approve or reject requests based on availability.Blood Distribution / Issue ManagementIssue blood against approved requests.Update inventory after blood distribution.Maintain blood issue and transaction history.Blood Availability SearchSearch available blood by blood group.Display current blood-unit availability.Expiry ManagementMaintain blood collection and expiry information.Identify expired blood units.Prevent expired units from being issued.User / Admin ManagementProvide secure login for blood-bank staff/admin.Allow authorized users to manage donors, stock, and requests.Reports & MonitoringGenerate donor, donation, inventory, request, and distribution reports.Display low-stock and expiry alerts.Data Storage & ValidationStore and retrieve records reliably.Validate donor details, blood groups, quantities, and request information.Prevent duplicate and invalid records.Static AnalysisStatic Analysis
